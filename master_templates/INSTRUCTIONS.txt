ONE METADATA UPLOAD - THREE SHEETS

DIVISION_MERCHANT:
  Fill MERCHANT for each DIVISION. One matching row per division.

ARTICLE_MASTER:
  Keys: DIVISION, SECTION, DEPARTMENT, ARTICLE NAME.
  Fill ART_STATUS, STH% MAMJ'26 and STH% JA'26. STH is used in Regular only.
  The legacy ATTRIBUTE column is ignored and may be omitted.

DEPARTMENT_MASTER:
  Keys: DIVISION, SECTION, DEPARTMENT.
  Fill ATTRIBUTE, SALE RANK, STH% MAMJ'26 and STH% JA'26.
  Sale Rank is fetched at Department level in both Department and Article reports.
  Department STH is used in Regular only. Imported fields are pasted as values.

Enter percentages as 12.5% or 0.125, not plain 12.5 for 12.5%.
Blank metadata remains blank. Duplicate keys are rejected. Add new categories as
needed. Metadata does not add report rows; the source/buy-plan union controls them.

UPDATED PPO TEMPLATE:
  Use PPO_DATA_Upload_Template_Schedule_DT.xlsx.
  SCHEDULE DT is Delivery Date; no separate DELIVERY DATE column is required.
  DELIVERY DATE is also accepted. If both columns are supplied, they must agree.
  October PPO includes October plus all earlier pending deliveries.
  November and December use their own Delivery Date month windows.
  Keep the other field names and enter raw data starting at row 2.
